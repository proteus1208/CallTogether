CallTogether

Settings

CallTogether captures speaker / tab / system audio (not your microphone), transcribes it locally on your machine, shows a floating live transcript on the AI page, and pastes it into chat with a hotkey.

Paste hotkey

Focus the AI chat composer, then press this shortcut to paste the transcript (no Enter). Use the paper-plane button to paste and send.

How to use

  1. Open your AI tab (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini…) — the floating panel appears and can be dragged freely.
  2. Toolbar popup → Desktop / app audio for Zoom or other apps (Entire Screen + system audio), or Chrome tab audio for a browser call tab.
  3. Watch the live transcript, focus the AI input, press your hotkey.

Initial AI prompts and CV / file uploads stay manual on the AI site. CallTogether only handles call audio → text → paste.
